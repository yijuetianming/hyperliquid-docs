# Accidentally transferred to HyperEVM

Description: You clicked the “Transfer to/from EVM” button and can’t figure out what happened

### How do I transfer it back to Spot? <a href="#transfer-back-to-spot" id="transfer-back-to-spot"></a>

* Your funds are not lost. They went from HyperCore to the HyperEVM. HyperCore and the HyperEVM are two parts of the Hyperliquid blockchain. You can visualize what that means here: <../../../about-hyperliquid.md>
* If you want to move your tokens back to Spot, you can follow this guide here: <../../../onboarding/how-to-use-the-hyperevm.md#how-do-i-move-assets-to-and-from-the-hyperevm>. You will need HYPE on the EVM for gas. If you transferred something other than HYPE to the EVM, you can buy HYPE on Spot and use the same “Transfer to/from EVM” feature to move HYPE to the EVM for gas.
* You can find out more about what you can do on the EVM here: <../../../onboarding/how-to-use-the-hyperevm.md#what-can-i-do-on-the-hyperevm>
