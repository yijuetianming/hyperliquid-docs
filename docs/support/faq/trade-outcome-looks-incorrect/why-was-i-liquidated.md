# Why was I liquidated?

Description: Your perps position was liquidated and you see "Market Liquidation" in your Trade History

### Reasoning

* A liquidation event occurs when a trader's positions move against them to the point where the account equity falls below the maintenance margin. You can read about how liquidations work here: <../../../trading/liquidations.md>
